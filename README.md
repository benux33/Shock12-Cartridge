# Shock12-Cartridge
The 12/70 Shock-12 is a fictional less-lethal shotgun cartridge designed to incapacitate targets through overwhelming blunt force instead of penetration or bleeding.
A successful hit deals 25 direct damage and applies several powerful temporary effects. The target experiences extreme hand tremors that make accurate aiming extremely difficult, prolonged pain, concussion and heavy stamina damage. A brief panic attack also forces the target’s currently equipped weapon to jam, creating an opportunity to retreat, reposition or finish the engagement.
Despite its strong incapacitating effects, Shock-12 has only 1 penetration power and cannot cause fragmentation, light bleeding or heavy bleeding. It is intended for disabling unarmoured targets and disrupting enemies rather than penetrating protective equipment or inflicting lethal wounds.
The cartridge uses a distinctive dark-blue 12-gauge shell, making it easy to identify in inventories, magazines and ammunition containers.
Cartridge statistics:
25 damage
1 penetration
0% fragmentation chance
0% light bleeding chance
0% heavy bleeding chance
Very high stamina damage
Extreme hand tremor for 15 seconds
Pain for 60 seconds
Concussion for 30 seconds
Panic-induced weapon jam for 5 seconds
Dark-blue 12/70 shell
Shock-12 can be used by players and may also appear in compatible Scav loadouts when Special Cartridge Scavs is installed.
